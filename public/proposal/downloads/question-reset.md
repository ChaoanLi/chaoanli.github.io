# Research question decision and public review record

# Two experiments for the faculty discussion

**Start with stock allocation in a thin aluminum plate (M26). Develop recycled polypropylene lot blending (M38) alongside it.** A machinist deciding whether to interrupt a cut can compare three ways of completing a real part on one mill. A compounder deciding whether to consume scarce material on a trial blend can test its value against a competent purchasing and routing rule. Both questions could yield a useful answer even when the extra measurement seldom pays. Neither has a confirmed decision owner, staffed pilot or observed advantage.

## The plate gate

**The closest prior result already changes upper and lower machining allowances using measured deformation history.** Li and colleagues also developed a fixture that responds to workpiece deformation, and GOALI 1762722 pursued stress-informed tool paths to reduce assembly costs. The new question asks which otherwise comparable histories warrant a physical interruption and whether the information repays the associated disturbance. Confirm an operator on one mill, several traceable parent plates, a drawing permitting both splits and a free-state flatness gauge. The team demonstrated instrumented MR-1 motion. A shared Haas listing adds no requirement for this pilot. [Hao et al. on adaptive allowances](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cja.2020.03.038) · [Li et al. on stock bounds](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cirp.2015.04.025) · [GOALI 1762722](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/1762722.json) · [MR-1 experiment](https://imece.secure-platform.com/a/solicitations/281/sessiongallery/24701/application/192618)

Before collecting outcomes, specify the minimum worthwhile difference in **unsigned final flatness error or conformance**. Opposite bow directions alone can leave two plates equally far from the drawing. On siblings, require a reversal in which split improves the endpoint. On reserved parents, use uninterrupted history-only machining as the primary operational comparator and the identically handled blind arm to isolate information value. Predefine how jobs will be weighted and charge re-datuming, measurement and missed production. Other fixed or reduced-layer-removal rules have prospective support only where the trial executes their actions. A sufficiently precise bound identifying jobs where stopping cannot recover its cost would be a scientific result. An interval spanning costly failure and substantial benefit leaves the pilot inconclusive.

Across three years, Bukkapatnam establishes the treatment and independent grade, Tuo selects stock conditions where competing stress profiles predict different choices, and Zhang estimates conditional split effects and validates the resulting stop policy on independent parents. The case for three investigators weakens if a single history-only split works throughout.

Use the shop’s best feasible support span and release sequence as the starting fixture practice. A laser study of thin plates found appreciable release effects for the larger plates in its setup. Artificially increasing the support span to induce a failure would undercut the industrial case. If a routine fixture correction solves the drawing problem, redirect the pilot. [Multistage laser measurements](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00170-026-17756-9)

## The blend gate

**An industrial-data batch optimizer already predicts properties from constituent assays and chooses recycled PP mixtures.** A study of eleven industrial feedstocks also provides a candidate rheological assay of phase interactions. A funded Black & Decker–Akron project pursued compatibilizer selection and predictive quality control under variable recycled feedstocks. Keep additives fixed, give an optimized constituent policy the same grams, staffed time and deadline, and test what a trial of the mixture buys. SoMF offers microcompounding, gram-scale molding and tensile testing. Virgin PP can qualify the equipment. To test the hypothesis, obtain traceable independently produced recyclate lots, independent recompounding and a decision owner’s written specifications and shortage priorities for two orders. Separate sacks and tensile bars from one charge cannot supply independent source lots or independent charges. [Teruel et al. on industrial batch optimization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2023.140390) · [Industrial feedstock experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42105589/) · [Funded compatibilizer project](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/2022-12/2022%20Cohort%20Track%20I%20Phase%201%20Award%20Announcements%20-%20Black%20%26%20Decker%20%28u.s.%29%20Inc.508.pdf?VersionId=tbTzD1PWRa7wQeXbnMKWbxbh8svj.Qig) · [TAMU core and rates](https://somf.engr.tamu.edu/fees/)

First require a consequential departure from the locally calibrated constituent rule that repeats on independently remixed charges. Freeze the feasible routes initially to the tested pure feeds and one interior fraction. Assign matched physical subinventories and order-to-stock ratios to each policy, charge assay and purge losses, and specify minimum charge size. On disjoint unseen lot pairs, execute the chosen routes, independently grade the outputs, and report qualified mass by order, shortage frequency, changeover time and owner-valued loss. A production-scale reversal claim requires testing both competing routes at both scales on the same reserved subset. A gram-scale laboratory specimen cannot establish the economics of a processor's line. A tight upper bound on the gain from a trial under several relevant order conditions could justify the work. One anomalous blend or one failed significance test could not.

Bukkapatnam controls sampling, melt history and grade measurement. Tuo selects a composition where rival models change an attainable order allocation. Zhang separates charge variability from pair effects and designs validation for shared constituent lots. **Move blending to first place** if the processor and order owner commit to the experiment, the trial changes feasible routing after losses, and the plate experiment yields no consequential reversal or costs more than it can save. If neither first experiment clears its gate, the practical reserve needs a permissive, costly hold and the scientific reserve needs demonstrable attributable shedding before either warrants a new four-page proposal.

# Seven questions reviewed on the same terms

Each card identifies the decision, the unresolved scientific claim, a competitive baseline, a three-year division of labor and an observation that would end or change the project. All equipment allocations and industrial participation are prospective.

## Stock allocation in thin-plate machining

**Decision and need.** While an aluminum plate is clamped, thickness can conform although the released plate will bow. Is one interruption to measure free shape worth its time while enough stock remains to change the finish split?

**Knowledge and rival.** The testable claim is that the new profile predicts the *difference between the two feasible cuts*, conditional on information already collected. Hao et al. already adjusted allowances from deformation history. Li et al. established stock limits. NSF 1762722 studied machining-path compensation. A conventional history-based allocation gets the same information and a trained operator.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam establishes traceable parent blanks and a gauge independent of the workholding fixture. Tuo chooses thickness/overhang or stock conditions where physically plausible stress profiles favor different splits. Zhang sizes blocked randomizations, keeps whole parents aside and tests the resulting policy across setups. The released-shape measurement, sham reseat and normal uninterrupted operation distinguish information value, handling and final shop benefit.

**First decision.** Show a drawing that permits both cuts and a repeat-release variation smaller than a consequential flatness difference. If plausible opposite allocations on held-out siblings lead to essentially equal conformance, retaining uninterrupted cutting is the result. Scope begins with one staffed, qualified mill. Paper outputs would concern potential identifiability and the conformance/time frontier. [Allowance control](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cja.2020.03.038) · [Stock bound](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cirp.2015.04.025) · [NSF 1762722](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/1762722.json) · [Team MR-1 experiment](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7243783)

## Compatibility of recycled polymer feedstocks

**Decision and need.** A compounder must allocate two incoming polypropylene lots to customer orders, blend them, or buy a small trial before committing stock. Industrial lots ordered to similar specifications can differ in measured properties. That alone does not establish a pair effect.

**Knowledge and rival.** Ask whether a pair-specific contribution to ductility predicts an otherwise missed routing failure after accounting for constituent properties and compounding variation. A published multi-property batch optimizer and an empirical constituent-only rule can spend the same grams and test minutes on more individual assays. An NSF Convergence Accelerator contract addresses variable recycled PP and compatibilizer selection. It is an adjacent program.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam establishes repeatable compounding, molding and mechanical reference tests. Tuo selects ratios at which plausible mixing models make different decisions. Zhang partitions lot, charge and specimen variance and holds both constituents of a validation pair out of fitting. The final experiment compares qualified mass across two orders, including testing, rejected material and other product constraints.

**First decision.** Secure independently sourced traceable recyclate lots and two meaningful product specifications. Independently recompound the same pair before interpreting an anomalous bar. Stop if charge noise or constituent-only calibration explains the residual. A null result can bound where additional pair trials are economically redundant. ## Retention of hidden burrs after cleaning

**Decision and need.** Manufacturers of intersecting fluid passages sequence drilling, deburr and wash. The added decision is whether a change of sequence or a targeted deburring pass prevents later shedding under specified flow while maintaining the edge tolerance.

**Knowledge and rival.** For lips with similar visible height, does attachment geometry predict subsequent release beyond pre-existing root damage and loose chips? Existing suction deburring can leave stable burrs. NSF 1911361 studied how plasma removes burrs without substrate damage. Give an optimized production deburr-and-wash cycle full credit.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam makes single-junction coupons and registers edge images against filters and sacrificial sections. Tuo models root loading and selects a flow direction or level that changes the predicted order of release. Zhang designs coupon-level contrasts and deals explicitly with imperfect particle attribution. Test the resulting operation choice on a new hole-size ratio with the same cleanliness and edge criteria.

**First decision.** Demonstrate an attributable lost fragment above the blank-circuit background at manufacturer-relevant flow, after ordinary cleaning. Include smooth passages and seeded recovery checks. No attributable release or no resolvable root or optical change ends the case. A source for inspection, the circuit, and an owner-defined cleanliness target are open. [Manufacturer workflow](https://www.sunhydraulics.com/sites/default/files/media_library/tech_resources/PB_US_Cleanliness-Jan2022.pdf) · [Suction-deburring study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cirp.2016.04.085) · [NSF 1911361](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/1911361.json)

## Predicting defect exposure after hybrid machining

**Decision and need.** A repair owner might refer a deposited-steel part for further inspection before machining. The useful endpoint is the complete finished face under a written rule. Existing virtual machining already predicts geometric exposure.

**Knowledge and rival.** Test whether a directional change of the original rim predicts a consequential grade beyond an achieved-depth geometric slice and a fitted correction. Adhesion, an existing overhang or registration error could give the same visual impression. Finish turning already changed measured porosity in powder-bed 316L.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam independently registers and sections randomized cutting conditions, including samples of apparent agreements. Tuo chooses a condition where a geometric prediction and a displaced-rim prediction disagree. Zhang reserves builds for face-level testing under known verification probabilities. Use pre-cut quantities only at the point of referral; assess all routes on comparable reference grades.

**First decision.** Obtain a repair decision, demonstrate near-threshold registration and rim sectioning, and separate new tasks and costs from funded award 2328395. A null directional result could define a geometric validity range. Its three-year operational value depends on a sufficient number of consequential faces. [Steel study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmapro.2025.10.080) · [Finish-turning precedent](https://doi.org/10.3390/jmmp2020036) · [Current team-related funding](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/2328395.json) · [AM comparator](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/2624720.json)

## Releasing molded lots during secondary shrinkage

**Decision and need.** A processor can wait a fixed conditioning time, release a lot after an early reading, or wait for another observation. A drawing owner must permit a timing change and define the final reference age.

**Knowledge and rival.** At equal early dimensions and temperatures, can different molding histories produce different late grades? A second reading may only measure a conventional shrinkage slope. Compare a calibrated time curve, fixed guard band and ordinary hold at the same permitted false-release rate. Published conditioning experiments and funded crystallization modeling already establish continued shrinkage.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam establishes a traceable molding and common isothermal holding experiment. Tuo selects histories, thicknesses and reading times where cooling-only predictions and latent-state predictions diverge. Zhang designs lot-level validation under adaptive observation. Freeze a rule before testing against the eventual dimension of every specimen, including those the policy would have released.

**First decision.** Establish repeatable late drift across independent days and lots, at a scale relevant to a buyer who could release earlier. A fixed guard band attaining the same time and error ends the adaptive design. An inexpensive observability trial cannot certify a small failure probability. [Conditioning study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7793073/) · [NSF crystallization comparator](https://api.nsf.gov/services/v1/awards/1653629.json) · [Molding equipment](https://somf.engr.tamu.edu/injection-molder/)

## Interchangeability after selective assembly

**Decision and need.** A supplier can issue universal or graded spares. If all unchanged housings ship and the future spare is independent, factory pairing alone cannot change service fit. A grade label or a change in stock must alter the service action.

**Knowledge and rival.** At a fixed number of spare grades, does an additional contact measurement identify housings for which diameter-only grouping recommends a poor replacement? Optimized binning and ordering are strong prior methods. Compare under the same spare inventory, assembly orientations and total test budget.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam fabricates or procures low-force sliding pairs, recording play, force, remounting and wear. Tuo models contact at the service orientation and selects informative pairings. Zhang accounts for reuse of a component across pairs and reserves whole housing and spare lots. Score both initial and replacement fit plus stranded units.

**First decision.** Find a real spare policy, attainable gauges and an orientation rule a technician can execute. Stop if ordinary diameter grades achieve the same performance. Experiments on fresh parts alone support no claim about worn field housings. ## First-part conformance after a mill change

**Decision and need.** A short-run shop moving a job to another mill must decide how many prove-out cuts to make. A compliant wall may respond differently from a stiff reference feature. Required first-article inspection remains in every arm.

**Knowledge and rival.** Ask whether the reference cut transfers outside a measurable range of tool and wall compliance. Compare a calibrated offset, measured compliance sum, measured tool dynamics and one target cut against a budget-matched target-only policy. Cross-system learning by itself already has a funded precedent in metal AM.

**Three groups, three years.** Bukkapatnam varies tool overhang and wall thickness independently on each machine, measuring runout, load and finished features. Tuo selects a target coupon whose predicted route differs under the competing models. Zhang reserves fresh setups and geometries, tests both transfer directions and measures extra coupons per conforming first article.

**First decision.** Demonstrate two staffed compatible loaded cuts and a repeatable residual beyond the calibrated rule. The group's MR-1 motion experiments establish a starting platform. A shared Haas inventory entry adds a potential second platform. With one working mill only the load mechanism can be tested. Two mills would support claims about that pair. # Selection protocol

**Decision.** Which two or three questions merit a three-group, three-year scientific discussion for the team's March 2027 target? A candidate can be rejected, retained, or replaced by an earlier or new idea. No rank carries forward.

1. Three originators independently examine material removal, other physical processes, and manufacturing operations. Start from a consequential decision and a feasible manipulation. Name the closest technical result, a funded precedent, which resources are demonstrated, and what the evidence does not establish. Add the three previous lead/contender questions explicitly if an originator did not include them.
2. The coordinator records every idea and its scientific disposition. Select seven contrasting questions for same-format project cards. Keep physically distinct explanations or interventions; merge near-duplicates with an explicit reason. Screen on generalizable knowledge, strongest practical or physical rival, independent measurement, an affordable first discrimination, and a meaningful three-group handoff. Confirmed machine use, inventory listing, external collaboration and proposed access are different levels of evidence.
3. Prepare anonymous abstracts for seven concepts and three track-relevant funded precedents, using the same editor, voice and approximate word budget, no titles, investigator credentials or award labels. Shuffle order and codes separately where used. The key is outside the packet. Two separate readers see only their packets. Each returns a top group, decisive evidence, concerns and one falsification. No additive funding score. This compares descriptions of scientific ideas, not full historical applications. Authors have richer information on local candidates. Short summaries can still reveal origin.
4. Freeze and archive the initial submissions and ballots. Translate the criticisms into specific changes or decisions to drop an idea. An independent challenge reads revised questions against their strongest practical rivals, without the previous ballots or investigator list. Funded descriptions stay historical. No final win over an unchanged award abstract is inferred from revisions offered only to our own ideas.
5. Reintroduce physical access, overlap with funded or published work, wider benefit and group dependencies. Make a conditional selection and state what information could overturn it. A favorable short-text reading cannot establish site access, technical novelty or a grant success rate. Archive all three origins, seven cards, anonymous packets, raw reader responses, the key and dispositions. Keep earlier drafts and votes as a dated archive.

# Reader instructions and masking limits

Two fresh AI sessions received different exhibit codes and shuffled orders of the same ten anonymous 78-word descriptions. Both had a maximum of four discussion slots and equal assumed resources. **Discussion order** — 1. X72, 2. X37, 3. X32, 4. X18.

X72 earns my first discussion slot because reversing tool travel offers a specific physical prediction. A rim displaced by cutting should change its position relative to the pore in a way that follows the tool direction. A depth error can change the apparent opening without producing that directional signature. A result that transfers across builds could establish when a subsurface scan is sufficient to predict finished-face quality and when the cutting operation must enter that prediction.

X37 is close. Identifying the attachment that survives washing yet later fractures could explain a hazard that a height limit misses. Its inference is harder because the finishing route can change both attachment shape and the damage already present at the root. Even a fragment traced to the correct site leaves open which of those changes governed release. X32 and X18 merit discussion because each asks when one measurement is worth interrupting production or shortening a hold. Both offer a research question beyond fitting a predictor if the experiments identify the physical conditions under which the measurement changes the best decision.

# Seven revised experiments

**M14.** In injection-molded, semicrystalline parts, compare samples at the same early dimension and temperature that have different molding histories. Follow them through a common isothermal hold to a drawing-specified reference age and measure separate thermal and material-state indicators on sacrificial siblings. Calibrate a predictor that can defer and ask for one more timed reading. Compare release time at matched false-acceptance risk with a fitted time curve, fixed guard band and standard hold using new lots and days; all parts receive the final grade. No claim of material-state identification follows from two dimensions alone.

**M38.** A compounder can sacrifice inventory for a test before allocating two incoming recyclate lots. Cross independent lots rather than just mixing one unusual pair. Randomize processing order, independently recompound each selected pair, and separate within-charge specimen error from between-charge variation. Give an optimized constituent-only recipe and a trial-blend policy identical material and assay budgets. Each must choose a route before independent reference charges are tested. Hold out both constituent lots together; quantify qualified mass lost or recovered across two product specifications, and account for properties traded off against ductility. End the case if processing noise or a conventional calibration explains the apparent pair interaction.

**M26.** On parent-blocked sibling blanks use one common rough cut, establish admissible finish splits and measure released shapes. Compare causal split effects on siblings with similar profiles, including cases whose stress models predict opposite best cuts. On reserved parent plates, precommit the decision to stop before the operator sees the reading. Randomize continuous, sham-reseat and reading-visible routes. Compare with the existing history-based allowance rule, including probe and reseat time and independent free-state grades. Report an equivalence interval or an inconclusive interval where the two cuts cannot be distinguished. Flatness alone supports predictive choice, not attribution of residual stress sources.

**M63.** Compare machining sequences on independent single-junction coupons with identical materials, bore sizes and industrial deburring. Vary finishing within drill order, randomize flow exposure, and compare against an optimized commercial deburr-all route. Control free swarf with circuit blanks, smooth passages and recovery checks. Relate disappearing edge segments to recovered particles; report ambiguous matches and background separately. Measure pre-existing root tears and attachment thickness on sacrificial siblings. Mechanistic attribution requires a release contrast that survives changes of realistic flow direction or load within comparable root-damage and size groups. Benefit requires fewer cleaning or machining minutes at the same outgoing cleanliness and edge limits.

**M71.** On each of two confirmed mills use a common three-axis operation; independently vary wall thickness and tool overhang across jobs in both transfer directions. Inspect tool runout, response and finished errors. Baselines include a measured compliance sum, full calibration, one target reference cut and a budget-matched target-only method. Reserve setup days and feature combinations. Compare added coupons while holding first-article conformance and mandatory inspection constant. A one-machine replication tests a mechanism but cannot establish transfer. A fleet-wide rule cannot be inferred from two mills.

**M47.** Milling can move the rim of a pore in deposited steel. On independent builds register achieved cut planes and bound imaging error. Reverse tool travel, record displacement of each edge in a fixed frame, and check for fragments, adhered material, or overhang on blinded sections of agreements as well as errors. Section-selected sites have known probabilities. Test whole-face grades under the owner's rule, alongside continuous widths, against a geometry/depth model and simple offset on reserved builds. Compare referral decisions under matched work and verification. An unresolved grade should trigger further inspection. The steel experiment already funded elsewhere needs a documented separation of new scope and costs.

**M52.** Factory matching alone cannot change the fit distribution of an independent spare if all unchanged housings ship. Test the distinct decision whether adding a contact-based grade to the housing and spare can improve service replacement. Machine or acquire independent batches, randomize pair and orientation, and measure play, sliding force, remount scatter and wear. Give an optimized diameter-only grade policy and the contact policy equal numbers of grades, inventory and testing resources. On fresh housing and spare lots, install according to a feasible service orientation rule without choosing the best angle after the fact. Report stock stranded and fit failures. Fresh components do not model aged field housings.

**M38.** The strongest benchmark in the set is the optimized constituent-only policy with the same material and assay budget, followed by independent reference charges from pairs for which both lots were held out. First test whether the difference between observed and conventionally predicted blend performance repeats across independent charges of the same pair, at a magnitude that changes allocation. A trial measurement can earn its cost by correcting an ordinary calibration error. That result would support an assay policy while retiring the pair-interaction thesis. The remaining depth question is what measurable property of unfamiliar constituents predicts when the interaction matters. Without that advance, the program could end after demonstrating the value of one extra test.

**M47.** Moving the cut plane changes the apparent pore opening even if the rim never moves. A registered geometry/depth predictor with a fitted offset is therefore the benchmark to beat. Reversing tool travel supplies a directional prediction for displacement of the two edges in a fixed frame. Blinded sections of predicted successes as well as failures can distinguish displacement from adhered material or a missing fragment. The gap is whether displacement exceeds registration error often enough to change whole-face grades. I would drop M47 if the geometry benchmark absorbed the directional signal on reserved builds, or keep it as physics alone if the surviving signal never changed a referral.

**M26.** The existing history-based allowance rule is the decision benchmark. Sham reseating is the decisive experimental control. The hard test is whether the observed profile selects a better finish split *within* a parent plate when credible models disagree. The proposed randomization helps distinguish information gained from the act of stopping and reseating. The remaining question is whether sibling-to-sibling variation is small enough to resolve a reversal in preferred split. A pooled difference in average flatness could merely identify one generally better cut. I would displace M26 if a fixed split performed as well across the disagreement cases or if sibling variability left practically important reversals unresolved.

**M63.** The strongest benchmark is commercial deburr-all optimized to meet the same outgoing edge and cleanliness limits. Recovery checks and matched edge segments give the proposed release mechanism a credible test. Root damage measured on siblings can still miss the tear at the particular edge that sheds. A release contrast surviving adjustment on siblings does not settle that ambiguity. This is my first alternate. Direct evidence linking root condition at the shedding edge to release under a second realistic load, with a credible time saving, would put it ahead of M26.

**M14.** The strongest benchmark is the fitted time curve with a calibrated guard band, evaluated at the same false-acceptance risk and final reference age. The extra timed reading has a clear release decision attached. Equal early dimensions and temperatures can still conceal different temperature fields or material states, and sibling measurements do not establish which caused later drift in the graded part. A controlled change that separates their predictions, followed by improved release timing on new lots, would raise the scientific case.

**M71.** The budget-matched target-only calibration is the strongest operational benchmark. The measured compliance sum is the strongest physical one. Varying wall thickness and overhang can reveal where that sum fails, but a gain on two machines may be specific to their two setups. The proposal needs a prediction for the sign or size of a transfer error in a reserved feature combination. Repeatedly beating target-only calibration in both directions at the same first-article conformance would make the decision credible. Explaining which combinations permit transfer would give it scientific depth.

**M52.** The optimized diameter-only policy with equal grades, inventory and testing is the right benchmark. The fresh-lot test can falsify a claim about replacement in fresh components. It has much less leverage over the stated service question because wear can change contact and invalidate the housing’s assigned grade. I would reconsider it if the investigators specified a contact feature with a testable prediction across orientations and showed that an actionable grade retained value on representative worn housings after stranded stock was charged to the policy.

## M26 · When should a machinist interrupt a plate cut to measure its free shape?

**Pilot practicable subject to drawing and gauge.** Bukkapatnam’s group demonstrated motion instrumentation on the three-axis Langmuir MR-1. A single staffed mill suffices. A plate process owner must supply a drawing that admits both final planes, a traceable parent-stock source, and the time or scrap a corrective split could save. A trained operator must confirm stock envelope, fixture, repeatable free support and an independent flatness gauge. Price matched sibling blanks from several parent plates, setup time, blind grading and reseating. Haas access would add little to this first decision. NSF GOALI 1762722 already funded stress-aware tool-path compensation for aluminum parts. Hao et al. physically changed upper and lower allowances based on deformation history. Charge the new free-state observation its entire opportunity cost. A noisy flatness comparison or a fixed-datum drawing supports only a short experiment.

Read the two pairs below in isolation. For each, consider scientific reach, experimental discrimination and relevance of the decision to a manufacturer. Prefer one, tie, or defer. ## Metal machining

**K4.** Aluminum parts can distort when machining releases residual stress. This project would infer the original stress fields in wrought and deposited stock by combining inverse mechanics with direct stress measurements. It would compare contributions from the starting material with those from heat, wear and cutting, map the geometry of machined parts, and design cutting paths that compensate for the inferred fields. Tests on feature-rich parts would determine how far the method improves dimensional tolerances and supports assembly.

**K7.** Two finishing cuts can leave the same thickness yet produce different flatness after a plate is released. This project would identify stock histories in which the better cut reverses, using sibling blanks and competing stress models. An operator would then decide when to interrupt machining to measure the released plate. Comparisons among uninterrupted machining, a concealed reading and a visible reading would measure the effects of moving the plate and using the information on drawing yield and machine time.

## Recycled polypropylene

**V2.** A compounder must decide whether to keep two feedstock lots separate or mix them before filling two orders. This project would measure whether lot pairs have reproducible responses missed by constituent assays, identify pairs where such departures change a feasible allocation, and compare small trial blends with further constituent testing under equal budgets. **V9.** Variable polyethylene content in recycled polypropylene can reduce the performance of molded products. # Scientific reading of the two matched comparisons

**Metal machining — prefer K7.** The concealed-reading arm gives the experiment an unusually informative comparison. If both interrupted groups gain flatness, releasing and refixturing the plate may itself be responsible. If showing the reading raises drawing yield further, the operator has evidence for spending machine time on the measurement. A reversal in cut ranking across stock histories would be scientifically informative if the competing models predict different rankings before the blanks are cut. K4 has greater potential reach across geometries because it measures stress directly and uses the inferred field to design a path. I would switch to K4 if it predicted the direction and magnitude of distortion on a new part geometry accurately enough to correct the first part.

**Recycled polypropylene — prefer V2.** A blend can depart measurably from an assay-based prediction without changing where either lot should go. I would switch to V9 if composition-guided dosing repeatedly brought previously unusable incoming lots into specification at lower total cost than the compounder’s usual formulation.
