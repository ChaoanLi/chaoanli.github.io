# Candidate card — [decision under investigation]

**Question for the lead PI:** Should we [specific bounded next action]? **Current position:** [disposition, reason, date].

| Comparison | Fill with observable details |
|---|---|
| Demand | Who can act and when? What consequence do they observe? Cite the demand source and identify what remains inferred. |
| Current practice or simpler response | What happens now? What information, time and resources does it require? |
| Closest existing work | Give an award, paper or dataset ID plus locator. Which part of our question does it already answer? Which claim remains uncertain? |
| Counterexample | Describe an observation that would favor current practice or a competing explanation. What would change our mind? |
| Data unit | One row means ___. Independent replication occurs across ___. Repeated readings share ___. Which action, comparator, outcome and timestamps must join? Who holds the data and what use is permitted? |
| Handoff between PIs | Group A supplies [artifact, schema/units, due date]. Group B accepts it if [test], passes [output with uncertainty] to Group C, who uses it for [decision with baseline]. Record which roles and resources are confirmed. |
| Alternatives and permissions | If access fails, what public result or narrower claim survives? Which claims should be retired? |
| Lead PI handoff | What can be concluded today? What single check should be authorized, who owns it, when is the next decision, and what observation triggers it? |

**Evidence register:** [source ID • issuer • URL/DOI • version/locator • date read • rights • claim supported]. Mark source facts, interpretations and unknowns in separate sentences.
