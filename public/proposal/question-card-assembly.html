Interchangeability after selective assembly

Decision and need. A supplier can issue universal or graded spares. If all unchanged housings ship and the future spare is independent, factory pairing alone cannot change service fit. A grade label or a change in stock must alter the service action.

Knowledge and rival. At a fixed number of spare grades, does an additional contact measurement identify housings for which diameter-only grouping recommends a poor replacement? Optimized binning and ordering are strong prior methods. Compare under the same spare inventory, assembly orientations and total test budget.

Three groups, three years. Bukkapatnam fabricates or procures low-force sliding pairs, recording play, force, remounting and wear. Tuo models contact at the service orientation and selects informative pairings. Zhang accounts for reuse of a component across pairs and reserves whole housing and spare lots. Score both initial and replacement fit plus stranded units.

First decision. Find a real spare policy, attainable gauges and an orientation rule a technician can execute. Stop if ordinary diameter grades achieve the same performance. Experiments on fresh parts alone support no claim about worn field housings. Optimal grouping · Ordering precedent · Adjacent tolerance and assembly award

Experiment after review

Factory matching alone cannot change the fit distribution of an independent spare if all unchanged housings ship. Test the distinct decision whether adding a contact-based grade to the housing and spare can improve service replacement. Machine or acquire independent batches, randomize pair and orientation, and measure play, sliding force, remount scatter and wear. Give an optimized diameter-only grade policy and the contact policy equal numbers of grades, inventory and testing resources. On fresh housing and spare lots, install according to a feasible service orientation rule without choosing the best angle after the fact. Report stock stranded and fit failures. Fresh components do not model aged field housings.
