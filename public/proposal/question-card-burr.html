Retention of hidden burrs after cleaning

Decision and need. Manufacturers of intersecting fluid passages sequence drilling, deburr and wash. The added decision is whether a change of sequence or a targeted deburring pass prevents later shedding under specified flow while maintaining the edge tolerance.

Knowledge and rival. For lips with similar visible height, does attachment geometry predict subsequent release beyond pre-existing root damage and loose chips? Existing suction deburring can leave stable burrs. NSF 1911361 studied how plasma removes burrs without substrate damage. Give an optimized production deburr-and-wash cycle full credit.

Three groups, three years. Bukkapatnam makes single-junction coupons and registers edge images against filters and sacrificial sections. Tuo models root loading and selects a flow direction or level that changes the predicted order of release. Zhang designs coupon-level contrasts and deals explicitly with imperfect particle attribution. Test the resulting operation choice on a new hole-size ratio with the same cleanliness and edge criteria.

First decision. Demonstrate an attributable lost fragment above the blank-circuit background at manufacturer-relevant flow, after ordinary cleaning. Include smooth passages and seeded recovery checks. No attributable release or no resolvable root or optical change ends the case. Experiment after review

Compare machining sequences on independent single-junction coupons with identical materials, bore sizes and industrial deburring. Vary finishing within drill order, randomize flow exposure, and compare against an optimized commercial deburr-all route. Control free swarf with circuit blanks, smooth passages and recovery checks. Relate disappearing edge segments to recovered particles; report ambiguous matches and background separately. Measure pre-existing root tears and attachment thickness on sacrificial siblings. Mechanistic attribution requires a release contrast that survives changes of realistic flow direction or load within comparable root-damage and size groups. Benefit requires fewer cleaning or machining minutes at the same outgoing cleanliness and edge limits.
