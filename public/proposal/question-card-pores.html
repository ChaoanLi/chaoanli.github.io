Predicting defect exposure after hybrid machining

Decision and need. A repair owner might refer a deposited-steel part for further inspection before machining. The useful endpoint is the complete finished face under a written rule. Existing virtual machining already predicts geometric exposure.

Knowledge and rival. Test whether a directional change of the original rim predicts a consequential grade beyond an achieved-depth geometric slice and a fitted correction. Adhesion, an existing overhang or registration error could give the same visual impression. Finish turning already changed measured porosity in powder-bed 316L.

Three groups, three years. Bukkapatnam independently registers and sections randomized cutting conditions, including samples of apparent agreements. Tuo chooses a condition where a geometric prediction and a displaced-rim prediction disagree. Zhang reserves builds for face-level testing under known verification probabilities. Use pre-cut quantities only at the point of referral; assess all routes on comparable reference grades.

First decision. Obtain a repair decision, demonstrate near-threshold registration and rim sectioning, and separate new tasks and costs from funded award 2328395. A null directional result could define a geometric validity range. Its three-year operational value depends on a sufficient number of consequential faces. Experiment after review

Milling can move the rim of a pore in deposited steel. On independent builds register achieved cut planes and bound imaging error. Reverse tool travel, record displacement of each edge in a fixed frame, and check for fragments, adhered material, or overhang on blinded sections of agreements as well as errors. Section-selected sites have known probabilities. Test whole-face grades under the owner's rule, alongside continuous widths, against a geometry/depth model and simple offset on reserved builds. Compare referral decisions under matched work and verification. An unresolved grade should trigger further inspection. The steel experiment already funded elsewhere needs a documented separation of new scope and costs.
