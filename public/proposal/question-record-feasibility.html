Equipment, people and funded overlap

Discuss the thin plate (M26) and the recycled-lot trial blend (M38) first. Bring post-mold release (M14) as the third question. M26 offers the shortest route from the team’s documented machining work to a new causal comparison. M38 has a purchaser’s decision, an industrial-data benchmark and a complete campus processing route. M14 has the cheapest credible test of observability, though the value and permissibility of early release remain open. M63 is the strongest reserve if a manifold producer can supply a representative cleaning cycle, a relevant flow challenge and evidence of residual shedding. These are recommendations for a PI discussion. No operator booking, participating manufacturer, specimen commitment or pilot result was established for any candidate.

M26 · When should a machinist interrupt a plate cut to measure its free shape?

Pilot practicable subject to drawing and gauge. Bukkapatnam’s group demonstrated motion instrumentation on the three-axis Langmuir MR-1. A single staffed mill suffices. A plate process owner must supply a drawing that admits both final planes, a traceable parent-stock source, and the time or scrap a corrective split could save. A trained operator must confirm stock envelope, fixture, repeatable free support and an independent flatness gauge. Price matched sibling blanks from several parent plates, setup time, blind grading and reseating. Haas access would add little to this first decision. NSF GOALI 1762722 already funded stress-aware tool-path compensation for aluminum parts. Hao et al. physically changed upper and lower allowances based on deformation history. Charge the new free-state observation its entire opportunity cost. Team MR-1 experiment · GOALI 1762722 · Adaptive allowance controller

Decision and three-year case. Prespecify an engineering equivalence margin. Begin with sham release/reseat measurements and random opposing feasible cuts on siblings. Retire if the drawing fixes the midplane, handling scatter swamps actionable change, or the augmented rule cannot recover enough reject cost to cover the stop. For three PIs, Bukkapatnam must reproduce a decision-changing physical contrast, Tuo must identify where competing deformation models prescribe opposite cuts, and Zhang must estimate that contrast and uncertainty on reserved parent plates after the region and stop rule have been selected. A transferable account of where a reading has negligible value, with narrow bounds and replication across stock conditions, can justify a program. A noisy flatness comparison or a fixed-datum drawing supports only a short experiment.

M38 · Is a trial blend worth sacrificing material before routing two recycled PP lots?

Pilot practicable subject to feedstock provenance. Teruel et al. used a recycler’s historical input/output batch data to develop a multi-property mixing rule. A producer explicitly offers lot-level laboratory results and identification documents. These establish that the workflow and identified lots exist commercially. Neither establishes TAMU supply, a pair-specific interaction or a customer's willingness to purchase a trial. Source independently produced recycled lots with lot IDs, provenance, available mass, and later arrivals for validation. Multiple bags drawn from one lot are one lot. Virgin PP is useful for commissioning the apparatus and measuring repeatability. It cannot answer the question about heterogeneity in recyclate. SoMF lists a microcompounder, MiniJet and tensile tester. Internal self-use/assisted hourly rates are $28/$83, $28/$83 and $35/$90, respectively. Quote training, purge, drying, destructive assays, scrap, receipt and storage of lots, and time for independent recompounding of reference charges. Name a compounder who owns both order specifications and can say which properties and inventory are binding. Industrial mixing algorithm · Producer batch identification · TAMU instruments · TAMU rates

Decision and three-year case. NSF contract 49100423C0004 already targeted quality and cost under varying recycled PP/PE composition by choosing compatibilizers. An eleven-feedstock study offers a stronger baseline than melt-flow rate alone through rheological measures of interfacial behavior. Give constituent assays that option within the same budget. Bukkapatnam needs a polymer-processing collaborator or operator who can distinguish processing artifacts from chemistry. Tuo selects a ratio at which rival models yield different routing. Zhang needs to solve the design and validation problem created by lot pairs that share constituents, including a final test on pairs with both constituent lots unseen. The three-year case depends on trials that change allocation after accounting for between-charge variability and sacrificed inventory. A tight cross-lot bound on the maximum gain from trial information could justify funding if it transfers across decision-relevant specifications and lots. A demonstration on one curious pair, or ductility improved at the expense of a binding impact or flow requirement, removes M38 from the shortlist. Funded compatibilizer project · Industrial recyclate study

M14 · When can a molder release a part before its reference age?

Pilot practicable subject to a credible reference age and gauge. A controlled polypropylene experiment measured late shrinkage and dependence on molding history. NSF 1653629 funded flow-induced crystallization and injection-molding simulation. Measuring another shrinkage curve would add little. SoMF’s MiniJet accepts gram-scale charges and controlled mold settings. New users can request a training or assisted session. A single documented virgin PP grade can isolate thermal histories for this pilot. Reserve independent resin lots and molding days for transport claims. Internal rates are $28/hour self-use or $83/hour assisted for each of the MiniJet and DSC. Price the mold, calibrated dimensional gauge, controlled hold, repeat measurements and destructive siblings. A quality or assembly owner must produce the drawing, reference age, permissible early action, actual queue or holding cost and tolerable false-release risk. A published conditioning protocol establishes a measurement time. It supplies no evidence of paid delay, an acceptance waiver or shipments waiting for the gauge. Original PP experiment · Funded crystallization work · MiniJet · SoMF booking · Rates

Decision and three-year case. Test whether parts at comparable early dimension and temperature diverge enough by the reference age to change a decision. Compare a log-time fit, fixed guard band and standard hold with the rule that requests one more timed observation. Bukkapatnam must control histories and establish the independent state measurements. Tuo must find when an additional time point resolves materially different late predictions. Zhang must bound false releases after adaptive deferral under independent lot and day variation. A two-day pilot estimates repeatability. Rare-error claims require enough independent lots and releases. A theory and measured boundary showing when early release is impossible at a specified risk would be substantial if it survives new conditions. Evidence of a contractually fixed hold with no permitted intermediate action, near-zero holding cost, or a guard band matching the rule removes M14 from the discussion.

M63 · Which residual drilled edges shed metal after a manifold is cleaned?

Multiple unverified dependencies, with the clearest named factory workflow. Sun Hydraulics documents operation sequencing, manual cutting of intersecting edges, washing and periodic cleanliness checks. Its stated cleanliness measure concerns residual contamination under its own protocol. It does not identify a failure rate under a subsequent flow challenge. Commercial cross-hole deburring provides a serious comparator. TAMU lists CNC machining, SEM/EDS and metallographic preparation. The manufacturer or hydraulic test engineer must supply a representative washing and flow envelope, outlet and edge specifications and current minutes per accepted part. Obtain quotes for an internal-viewing optic, filtered circuit, flow measurement, commercial deburr tools, sacrificial sections and operator time. IMS posts $50/hour for TAMU SEM use and $50/hour for standard polishing or $10/hour for trained self-polishing. The imaging of an intact junction remains unverified. NSF 0300549 investigated drilling simulation, while AM 1911361 investigated flow–burr interactions during active laser-plasma removal. The proposed distinction is release of an attached lip under subsequent service-relevant flow after standard deburring. Sun process bulletin, p. 4 · Commercial deburring · GOALI 0300549 · AM 1911361 · IMS rates

What would move it up. After a credible incumbent cycle, recover compatible particles from visible disappearing edge sites above circuit blanks at relevant loads. Document an informative change of rank with finishing or flow, reliable recovery, ordinary spurious swarf and enough cycle minutes to matter. Bukkapatnam owns preparation and release attribution, Tuo must predict how a change of load discriminates attachment models, and Zhang must propagate imperfect matching and destructive-sibling uncertainty through a coupon-level policy comparison. A cross-geometry bound on shedding after specified procedures could be valuable to an owner with a stated limit. All-zero filters without demonstrated detection and adequate independent coupons fund no three-year study.

M71 · How many commissioning cuts does a transferred milling job need?

Two-machine claim depends on two operators, compatible loaded cuts and an independent reference. The recent team MR-1 experiment establishes instrumented motion. IMS lists a separate Haas five-axis mill. Model, access windows, tooling compatibility and loaded accuracy on that Haas remain open. A process owner must identify a rerouted job and excess prove-out effort above compulsory first-article inspection. A supplier procedure supports the first-article obligation after relevant changes. Quote both machines, setup days, common fixtures, runout and compliance measurements, development coupons and blind final inspection. NSF 2046515 addresses cross-system defect-model transfer in deposition. NIST’s receptance-coupling experiments already predict changed tool response with overhang. An added coupon must beat measured-compliance, calibrated transfer, one-target-cut and equal-budget target-only policies. MR-1 experiment · IMS inventory · Supplier first-article procedure · Funded transfer project · NIST tool dynamics

What would move it up. Obtain staffed slots on both mills for the same cuts in both transfer directions and a finished-feature error greater than gauging uncertainty that changes the required commissioning decision. One-mill experiments support a compliance mechanism. They supply zero independent transfers. Bukkapatnam must characterize and change the physical setup, Tuo must select informative target cuts, and Zhang must estimate prospective policy loss on reserved setup days and wall/overhang combinations. An equivalence region on two machines could justify a focused study. A fleet-wide guarantee needs new independent machines, and stable offsets on two machines with no consequential rerouting burden leave little for three PIs.

M47 · Does finish milling move a pore rim enough to change the face grade?

Funded scope must be defined before proposal commitment. The team published a 316L deposition and milling study listed under ongoing NSF FMRG 2328395. The public award identifies Panganamala Kumar as PI and lists December 31, 2027 as its expiration. The award abstract includes adaptive experiments and multifidelity twins, and the prior paper explicitly addresses in-situ surface porosity prediction. Neither source proves that the proposed new rim-displacement experiments have already been paid for. Ask the award PI and proposed investigators to compare aims, existing specimens and data, committed builds, staff effort, machine/CT hours and budgets. Define incremental work in writing. AM 2624720 provides the adjacent comparison of sensing as-built defects. A repair user must supply one actual deposited 316L face with a relevant acceptance rule, pre-cut referral option, downstream cost, and enough routine rejects. IMS posts $200/hour TAMU for the Optomec, excluding materials, fixture and overhead. Quote CT on a representative steel section, blind serial sections, registration and review. A CT listing or hourly rate supplies no detectability guarantee. Team paper · FMRG award and publications · AM defect sensing · IMS fees · TAMU CT facility

What would move it up. After scope separation, show a direction-dependent signed rim shift exceeding depth and registration error, independently sectioned against plausible fragments and adhered material. Then show the owner's face grade changes, and the value of an avoidable downstream loss can cover the added referral costs at its routine frequency. Bukkapatnam owns registered machining and sections, Tuo owns discrimination against the geometry-only model from information available before cutting, and Zhang owns verification sampling across apparent agreements and errors with build-level uncertainty. A credible upper bound on grade changes may settle a costly validation decision. Without a decision owner or distinct funded effort, that negative result supports a metrology paper. A separate three-PI proposal would still need justification.

M52 · Would a contact-based spare grade improve replacement fit?

Bench fabrication is plausible. Service scope and incremental demand need definition. Zachry Labs accepts researchers’ part requests. A Yamaha service bulletin actually specifies a replacement bearing grade based on case and crankshaft markings and checks clearance. It provides a named precedent for an incumbent policy. The proposed fresh sliding shaft/sleeve geometry, frequency of failures, and a participating service counter still need independent evidence. NIST already describes size-class assembly. The nearest archived award, GOALI 1762722, is a partial comparator on tolerancing and assembly cost. Its intervention is upstream tool-path compensation. The service owner must supply part class, feasible orientation, friction/play limits, present grading cost and return/stock costs. Quote independently manufactured lots, dimensional/form gauges, load fixture, repeated mounting, controlled wear and reserve inventory. Campus fabrication · Yamaha service bulletin · NIST size-class case · GOALI 1762722

What would move it up. On more than one fresh component lot, demonstrate orientation-reproducible contact effects that change the ordering of viable grades under an installation method the service shop can perform. Keep inventory, number of grades and test cost equal for the best diameter-only alternative. Bukkapatnam owns the contact/wear experiment, Tuo needs a transportable calibration from measurable component features to assembled function, and Zhang needs inference for crossed housings/spares plus inventory and failure under a new lot. These roles collapse into a modest factorial study if diameter determines fit. Factory pairing cannot change a fresh independent replacement fit when all unchanged housings ship. Aged field claims require field housings or relevant controlled wear. The mathematical invariance alone and a fresh-parts null do not warrant three years.

What would justify three investigators?

The strongest argument is a sequence of decisions that depend on all three contributions. The manufacturing PI produces an independently graded perturbation or intervention. The calibration PI predicts the conditions where competing explanations imply different physical actions. The inference PI designs validation that accounts for the choice of where to look, shared material or setup, and the cost of abstaining. Each result informs the next experiment. A fitted model followed by routine held-out scoring would make one statistical role dispensable. The team’s existing calibration, multifidelity design and prespecified-region validation work establishes relevant expertise. It leaves the decision-dependent validation problem open. No candidate yet demonstrates enough physical signal, external demand and independent replication for an unconditional three-year commitment.
