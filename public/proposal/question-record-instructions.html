Reader instructions and masking limits

Two fresh AI sessions received different exhibit codes and shuffled orders of the same ten anonymous 78-word descriptions. Both had a maximum of four discussion slots and equal assumed resources. Reader A emphasized generalizable knowledge, physical discrimination and the capacity for sustained research. Reader B emphasized independent units, practical rivals and changes in actual decisions. Each was asked to identify a weakness and a defeating observation for every entry. They received only their own packet, without titles, funding labels, source URLs, team capabilities, author identities or the key. They are two sessions of the same model family. Disagreement combines reader framing and order effects.

The third reader received only the seven revised experiments under another set of codes, with no previous ballots. It rated discrimination, consequence for a decision and potential three-year scientific depth separately, from 1 (unspecified) to 5 (specified and falsifiable), with 3 meaning plausible with a material gap. The coordinator requested a maximum of three nominations, the strongest physics, the first useful experiment and displacement conditions. No totals were computed. One abstract mentions potential prior funding overlap; practical dependencies were more explicit in this round.

The practical reader received the seven revisions, the three unmasked design memoranda, team and facility evidence, and permission to check published sources. It was asked to examine opportunity cost, scope overlap, which people must participate, and the value of a null result, without access to comparative ballots. No applicant or program officer was contacted. Titles and webpages were written after these judgments. The exact masked packets and returned ballots are provided for inspection.

After the seven-project decisions, an independent reader saw the full four-page versions of the two surviving proposals. Those TeX files are preserved as read. After that reading, both projects were edited. A separate same-field comparison asked two fresh readers to compare four 77–79-word descriptions using the same anonymous packet in the same order. This additional comparison was devised after the finalists were known. It is susceptible to description, order and selection effects. The raw records distinguish the judgments from the subsequent changes. All reviewers in this exercise were AI simulations. NSF identifies intellectual merit and broader impacts as its formal criteria and restricts sharing confidential proposal information through public generative AI services.
