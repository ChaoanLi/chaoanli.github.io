Disposition of all 19 questions

The first pass produced 15 designs in three independent searches. A separate calibrated-metrology idea and the three directions on the prior homepage brought the total to 19. The seven admitted to the masked comparison included all three prior directions so that the comparison could overturn any of them. Four challengers cover a latent machining defect, a material-pair interaction, delayed release and service interchangeability. This purposeful screen does not estimate how a random pool of ideas would rank.

OriginProposed decisionDisposition at screening and reason
CNC 1Choose bore order or additional deburring to prevent shedding after washingAdvance. Named shop workflow, a rival physical explanation and a downstream endpoint distinct from burr height.
CNC 2Choose a peck-drilling retract schedule after a suspected chip obstructionHold. Optimized depth-based schedules and drill geometry are credible low-cost alternatives. Reopen if recovered chips predict a useful change above those baselines.
CNC 3Resume precision machining after a thermal interruptionHold. Existing fast thermal correction demonstrated on cut parts makes an extra intervention difficult to justify until a repeatable residual appears.
CNC 4Distinguish an insert fault from a pocket fault before replacing toolingHold. Index swaps can diagnose faults directly. Reopen if noise and downtime leave an economically important ambiguity after standard swaps.
CNC 5Change cutting conditions or index the edge when adhered material mimics wearHold. Existing team work establishes buildup effects. Need an inspection route distinguishing reversible buildup from permanent damage and a case where changing cutting conditions is practical.
Process 1Trial-blend two recycled polymer lots before committing a batchAdvance. A new lot pair is the independent decision, and the best alternative can receive the same grams and instrument minutes.
Process 2Sequence the restart of interrupted printed-mortar segmentsHold. Concrete access and comparable wall repeats are prospective. Cold-joint and strength precedents narrow the proposed advance.
Process 3Add a second programming cycle to a thermally activated printed clipHold. A demonstrable factory use, a permitted force–travel envelope and an actual processing choice are missing.
Process 4Stop proof-loading a bonded joint before the test damages a usable partHold. It could be reopened with a documented industrial proof procedure and conditioned residual-strength trials on matched batches.
Process 5Choose separate or contiguous seal bands for a flexible pouchHold. Attractive test of correlated leaks, but access to a traceable leak reference and a controlled contamination morphology is less developed.
Systems 1Release a molded batch now or wait for more shrinkage evidenceAdvance. Grounded fixed-hold alternative, a repeatable late grade and an available campus route to request time.
Systems 2Ship universal or graded spares after selective assemblyAdvance. A precise zero-benefit case constrains the claim. Design choices can be tested with fabricated components and fresh spare lots.
Systems 3Pack another order in a laser powder-bed buildHold. Physical interference is plausible; comparable independent builds and part-level defect measurements require core access and costly replication.
Systems 4Assign a scarce inspection slot before an irreversible operationHold. The value depends on a real queue with intervention windows, alternative jobs and observable counterfactual routing.
Systems 5Choose specimens for agreement between supplier and customer gaugesHold. Strong metrology baseline. Requires paired instruments and an acceptance decision that currently disagrees.
Independent metrology searchDetermine when a master-part correction needs an independent checkHold. A published comparator already corrects systematic effects. Retain its calibrated-artifact baseline for related designs.
Earlier proposalPredict grade-changing pore displacement after hybrid millingAdvance for direct comparison. Strong mechanism, expensive site registration and substantial funded-scope uncertainty.
Earlier proposalUse one free-state measurement to allocate the final thin-plate cutsAdvance for direct comparison. One-mill design with a credible identical-handling control; adaptive allowance methods already exist.
Earlier challengerPredict first-part accuracy after substitution of a milling machineAdvance for direct comparison. Strong transfer question; both compatible loaded machining setups and independent references remain prospective.

The originators’ memoranda include technical and funded precedents for the unscreened studies as well as the shortlisted ones. A future field contact, piece of equipment, or discriminating observation can change these dispositions.
