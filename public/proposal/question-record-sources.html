Public technical sources and award evidence

Industrial and national rationale. The AMDO page supports generalizable advances in materials processing, systems integration and analytical decision methods. A project may address a process or an operation. The CMMI solicitation encourages an early one-page fit discussion. March 2027 is the team's planning date. Neither broad policy language nor a recent technical term predicts panel preference. AMDO · NSF 26-515, especially sections II and VI

Industrial decisions. NIST describes difficulties establishing credible twins, especially for small and medium enterprises. Its on-machine measurement guide gives actual shop use cases and includes calibrated-artifact comparison. A recent machining study already combines Bayesian monitoring, nonintrusive sensing and on-machine comparator measurement. These references support asking when an additional check changes a manufacturing decision. They do not establish the prevalence or cost of failure in a particular shop. NIST digital twins · NIST use cases · Comparator study

Which physical observations are credible. The group's MR-1 study measures motion on a physical three-axis mill. Direct evidence of operation is stronger than a catalog entry. Published motion errors do not establish loaded workpiece tolerance. The IMS Haas five-axis mill is shared and listed separately. The Optomec LENS MTS 500 supports hybrid manufacturing. The 316L deposition-and-cut route is published; traceable pores on new builds, routine industrial face grades and staff availability are prospective. An older micromilling study gives direct experience with cutting-edge buildup on 316L. Historical access to external polishing, forming or production-line platforms needs fresh confirmation for a new trial. Team MR-1 preprint · MR-1 ASME experiment · IMS · 316L micromilling · Hybrid-route paper

What the three investigators contribute. Satish Bukkapatnam links cut, sensor and measured workpiece. Rui Tuo's calibration work addresses an imperfect simulator, and his multi-fidelity designs address spending a finite budget on different information sources. Xianyang Zhang's work covers dependence, change detection and inference. Their joint validation preprint concerns global or prespecified regions. A study that chooses the regions or decides when to stop using the observations needs a new validation design. Isolate setups, builds or batches as independent units. Reserve entire units for confirmation and compare decisions under the same observation budget. Calibration of imperfect simulators · Design across fidelities · Zhang et al. · Li, Zhang and Tuo

Grant and review evidence. The four predecessor searches match 1,655 institutional records; 1,306 have a predecessor managing code. Use a specific record and its actual scope as a comparator. Award 2328395 lists the team's hybrid steel study. New builds, measurements, people and machine time require an internal scope comparison. The 65-word round that favored machine transfer and the longer round favoring pores used different ballots and presentation. Both results are historical context only. Two readers running the same model family do not furnish an empirical funding rate. Team-related FMRG · Steel paper funding metadata · Prior ballots

Field evidence for the two finalists. A study of on-machine layer removal links variation in initial aluminum blank stress to a practical difficulty in choosing a process for ribbed structural parts. A separate 2026 plate experiment varied geometry and investigated unclamping during multistage measurement. These establish credible physical mechanisms and strong alternatives. A shop must still identify a drawing that permits a change in the final stock split and tell us what improvement would matter. A US Department of Energy workshop identified consistency of recycled feedstocks, matching material to end uses, and specifications as manufacturing barriers. Actual recycler data have already supported predictions for mixtures. This combination motivates a prospective experiment on the additional value of a trial blend against a well-calibrated constituent-only policy. Neither source provides the local orders or a prevalence estimate for the proposed reversal. On-machine layer removal · Multistage plate study · DOE workshop, supply-chain session · Industrial mixture prediction

What the primary measurements change. Casuso and colleagues reported up to 250 μm of shape change during unclamping for large-area plates in their tested setup. That maximum belongs to their fixtures and plates. It cannot size a TAMU effect. Start with the feasible shop fixture, support span and release sequence optimized for the real drawing. Do not create a large distortion by using an avoidably flexible fixture. If the optimized procedure already gives conforming parts throughout, drop the production claim. On polymers, Kianpisheh and colleagues measured eleven industrial feedstocks from different suppliers and production periods. Their results motivate a frequency-dependent rheology measurement of the constituents as an explicit competitor to the trial blend. The experiment must still show that enough pairs cross an operative order limit to justify testing. Plate experiments, pp. 2939 and 2950 · Industrial polymer measurements
