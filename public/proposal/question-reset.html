Choosing two three-year manufacturing projects

Decision for PI discussion

Start with stock allocation in a thin aluminum plate (M26). Develop recycled polypropylene lot blending (M38) alongside it. A machinist deciding whether to interrupt a cut can compare three ways of completing a real part on one mill. A compounder deciding whether to consume scarce material on a trial blend can test its value against a competent purchasing and routing rule. Both questions could yield a useful answer even when the extra measurement seldom pays. Neither has a confirmed decision owner, staffed pilot or observed advantage.

How the choices changed

Nineteen directions entered the initial screen, including all three leaders from the previous selection. Seven advanced. Two AI readers each considered the same ten anonymized 78-word exhibits, with different codes and orders. The comparison set included three actual funded projects on plasma deburring, multistage quality and equipment health, and void formation during composite molding. Readers could nominate up to four. The plate gate

The closest prior result already changes upper and lower machining allowances using measured deformation history. Li and colleagues also derived a remaining-stock salvage bound, and GOALI 1762722 pursued stress-informed tool paths to reduce assembly costs. The new question asks which otherwise comparable histories warrant a physical interruption and whether the information repays the associated disturbance. Confirm an operator on one mill, several traceable parent plates, a drawing permitting both splits and a free-state flatness gauge. The team demonstrated instrumented MR-1 motion. A shared Haas listing adds no requirement for this pilot. Hao et al. on adaptive allowances · Li et al. on stock bounds · GOALI 1762722 · MR-1 experiment

Before collecting outcomes, specify the minimum worthwhile difference in unsigned final flatness error or conformance. Opposite bow directions alone can leave two plates equally far from the drawing. On siblings, require a reversal in which split improves the endpoint. On reserved parents, use uninterrupted history-only machining as the primary operational comparator and the identically handled blind arm to isolate information value. Predefine how jobs will be weighted and charge re-datuming, measurement and missed production. Other fixed or reduced-layer-removal rules have prospective support only where the trial executes their actions. A sufficiently precise bound identifying jobs where stopping cannot recover its cost would be a scientific result. An interval spanning costly failure and substantial benefit leaves the pilot inconclusive.

Across three years, Bukkapatnam establishes the treatment and independent grade, Tuo selects stock conditions where competing stress profiles predict different choices, and Zhang estimates conditional split effects and validates the resulting stop policy on independent parents. The case for three investigators weakens if a single history-only split works throughout.

Use the shop’s best feasible support span and release sequence as the starting fixture practice. A laser study of thin plates found appreciable release effects for the larger plates in its setup. Artificially increasing the support span to induce a failure would undercut the industrial case. If a routine fixture correction solves the drawing problem, redirect the pilot. The blend gate

An industrial-data batch optimizer already predicts properties from constituent assays and chooses recycled PP mixtures. A study of eleven industrial feedstocks also provides a candidate rheological assay of phase interactions. A funded Black & Decker–Akron project pursued compatibilizer selection and predictive quality control under variable recycled feedstocks. Keep additives fixed, give an optimized constituent policy the same grams, staffed time and deadline, and test what a trial of the mixture buys. SoMF offers microcompounding, gram-scale molding and tensile testing. Virgin PP can qualify the equipment. To test the hypothesis, obtain traceable independently produced recyclate lots, independent recompounding and a decision owner’s written specifications and shortage priorities for two orders. Separate sacks and tensile bars from one charge cannot supply independent source lots or independent charges. Teruel et al. on industrial batch optimization · Industrial feedstock experiments · Funded compatibilizer project · TAMU core and rates

First require a consequential departure from the locally calibrated constituent rule that repeats on independently remixed charges. Freeze the feasible routes initially to the tested pure feeds and one interior fraction. Assign matched physical subinventories and order-to-stock ratios to each policy, charge assay and purge losses, and specify minimum charge size. On disjoint unseen lot pairs, execute the chosen routes, independently grade the outputs, and report qualified mass by order, shortage frequency, changeover time and owner-valued loss. A production-scale reversal claim requires testing both competing routes at both scales on the same reserved subset. A gram-scale laboratory specimen cannot establish the economics of a processor's line. A tight upper bound on the gain from a trial under several relevant order conditions could justify the work. One anomalous blend or one failed significance test could not.

Bukkapatnam controls sampling, melt history and grade measurement. Tuo selects a composition where rival models change an attainable order allocation. Zhang separates charge variability from pair effects and designs validation for shared constituent lots. Move blending to first place if the processor and order owner commit to the experiment, the trial changes feasible routing after losses, and the plate experiment yields no consequential reversal or costs more than it can save. If neither first experiment clears its gate, the practical reserve needs a permissive, costly hold and the scientific reserve needs demonstrable attributable shedding before either warrants a new four-page proposal.

Within-field comparison after selection

The first ten-exhibit field tested how ideas travel across specialties. After selection, two fresh AI readers received only a new four-exhibit packet, 77–79 words per exhibit. Each compared the thin-plate idea to the funded tool-path project in its own field, then compared the lot-routing idea to the funded compatibilizer project. The same editor paraphrased all four. The machining award is an Advanced Manufacturing search match with Manufacturing Machines and Equipment as its managing element. The polymer contract belongs to the Convergence Accelerator. Neither is a newly awarded AMDO proposal. Machining award 1762722 · NSF polymer project

Both preferred K7 (thin plates) and V2 (lot routing). Their strongest arguments for the competing projects expose two limits. Cutting paths derived from direct stress measurements could work across more geometries, particularly if a previously unseen part meets assembly tolerance on its first cut. An additive can recover material that assigning the same lots to different orders could never use. A prospective line test recovering more out-of-specification material at the allowable cost would favor that alternative. These post-selection judgments test how the proposed boundary reads. The first, independently coded ten-exhibit ballot remains the selection comparison. Actual panels had full proposals, applicant evidence and program-specific objectives. The final revisions to the four-page manuscripts have not received a new full-manuscript rating.
