Candidate comparison

Compare the nearest precedent, a simpler alternative and an observation that could distinguish them.

Question for the lead PI: Should we [specific bounded next action]? Current position: [disposition, reason, date].

ComparisonFill with observable details
DemandWho can act and when? What consequence do they observe? Cite the demand source and identify what remains inferred.
Current practice or simpler responseWhat happens now? What information, time and resources does it require?
Closest existing workGive an award, paper or dataset ID plus locator. Which part of our question does it already answer? Which claim remains uncertain?
CounterexampleDescribe an observation that would favor current practice or a competing explanation. What would change our mind?
Data unitOne row means ___. Independent replication occurs across ___. Repeated readings share ___. Which action, comparator, outcome and timestamps must join? Who holds the data and what use is permitted?
Handoff between PIsGroup A supplies [artifact, schema/units, due date]. Group B accepts it if [test], passes [output with uncertainty] to Group C, who uses it for [decision with baseline]. Record which roles and resources are confirmed.
Alternatives and permissionsIf access fails, what public result or narrower claim survives? Which claims should be retired?
Lead PI handoffWhat can be concluded today? What single check should be authorized, who owns it, when is the next decision, and what observation triggers it?

Evidence register: [source ID • issuer • URL/DOI • version/locator • date read • rights • claim supported]. Mark source facts, interpretations and unknowns in separate sentences.
